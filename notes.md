## Book Title: Oblismee

**Summary:**
Oblismee is a story set in a small mountain town in Colorado. The protagonist, Harriot, is the founder of the Widmont Alien Society. As a child, Harriot had an encounter with an alien, an experience that no one believed. Following this encounter, she began to suffer from mysterious illnesses that no one could explain. These illnesses had a profound effect on the woman she became.
